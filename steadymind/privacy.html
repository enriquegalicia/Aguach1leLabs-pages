SteadyMind — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SteadyMind collects nothing. Your scores and progress across all 20 challenges are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData and standard iOS storage. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No network access of any kind — every challenge runs entirely on your device

Children's privacy

SteadyMind is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
