Is my draw history backed up anywhere?

Your draws and favorites live only on your device. If you use iCloud backup for your phone, they're included in that backup like any other app's data. TheLuckyNumber itself has no cloud server.

Do the Zodiac / Birth Date / Probability draws actually use my input?

Your sign, date, or chosen lens is saved with the draw as a label, but every draw method uses the same fair, uniform shuffle under the hood — no method is "luckier" than another. See the app's disclaimer on every draw screen.

Something's not working

Email a description of what happened (and a screenshot if you can) to mat.enrique.galicia@gmail.com — include your iOS version and the app version (Settings).
