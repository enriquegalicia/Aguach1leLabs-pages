TheLuckyNumber — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

TheLuckyNumber collects nothing. Every number it draws, and every draw it saves to your history, is generated and stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData and standard iOS storage. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No network access of any kind — every draw is generated and logged entirely on your device

Entertainment only

TheLuckyNumber generates numbers for entertainment purposes only. It does not predict, and has no bearing on, any real lottery drawing.

Children's privacy

TheLuckyNumber is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
