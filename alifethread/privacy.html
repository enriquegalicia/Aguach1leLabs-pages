ALifeThread — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

ALifeThread is built local-first: your chapters, threads, and everything you write or say are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

ALifeThread requests microphone access so you can speak your reflections instead of typing them. Your voice is transcribed entirely on your device using Apple's on-device Speech framework — nothing you say is ever uploaded anywhere, not to Aguach1leLabs, not to any AI provider.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Children's privacy

ALifeThread is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
