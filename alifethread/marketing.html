LifeThread

Voice Journal

Talk to a version of you from another time — LifeThread is a place to reflect out loud, not just write it down.

What it does

  • Pick a voice: Past Me, Present Me, or Future Me — each opens with its own prompt
  • Speak your reflection, or type it — transcribed entirely on your device
  • Branch a "what if" thread any time to reflect from a path you didn't take
  • Your story builds as chapters, in your own words, over time

Your voice is transcribed entirely on your device — nothing you say is ever uploaded anywhere.
