TheRiseTrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

TheRiseTrack is built local-first: your routines and progress are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

TheRiseTrack requests microphone access so it can hear when you're ready to move to the next step during a voice-guided routine session, using Apple's on-device Speech framework. This never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Children's privacy

TheRiseTrack is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
