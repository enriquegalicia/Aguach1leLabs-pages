TheRiseTrack turns a new habit into a 30-day climb you can actually finish.

What it does

  • Pick your quests: choose a few daily quests from the library (wake up early, train 30 minutes, read 20 pages) or write your own. A quest can be a single tap, a counter or a timed session. Rest days never count against you
  • Climb for thirty days: every day you keep moves you along a 30-day arc in three phases, Foundation, Momentum and Automatic. Finish every quest to reach the day's summit
  • Never miss twice: every seven days you keep forges a Shield that absorbs a miss. Only a second miss in a row breaks the streak. Hard Mode is there if you want teeth
  • XP and levels: quests pay XP into five stats, and you level up through six tiers, from Trailhead to Peak
  • It adjusts to you: if a quest keeps slipping, it suggests a smaller target; if it's too easy, it offers a step up
  • A voice in your corner: voice-guided routines read your morning or evening steps out loud, hands-free

No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.
