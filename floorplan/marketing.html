The house

One of you is inside a dark house and has to find the key and get out before dawn. The other sees the house as a live blueprint: the key, the threat, and the doors, which they can open or close. Something in the house only moves while nobody inside is looking at it.

Two ways to play it

  1. Alone, with a bot partner who plays the other role: the person inside or the architect.

  2. Together on two iPhones or iPads on the same Wi-Fi: one hosts, the other types in a short code.

  3. Fifteen places on the City map: four easy, four normal, four hard and three expert.

Terms

  1. The first three levels are free, with every way to play.

  2. "The whole game" is one purchase that opens the other twelve, and every chapter added later.

  3. Your partner plays free: they join your game from the free download.

  4. No ads, no subscription, and no data collected. Family Sharing is on.

  5. Something stalks the house. It's tense and a little scary, with no gore.
