Notes

  1. The game

    A two-player co-op game. One of you is inside a dark house and has to find the key and get out before dawn. The other sees the house as a live blueprint: the key, the threat, and the doors, which they can open or close. Something in the house only moves while nobody inside is looking at it.

  2. Playing alone

    Yes. On the City map, choose a level and play as the person inside or the architect with a bot partner who plays the other role.

  3. Playing together

    Each on your own iPhone or iPad, both on the same Wi-Fi. On the City map, choose a level, then Play together: one phone each (on an iPad, Play online, on two devices). One of you taps Host a game and reads out the code; the other types it in and taps Join.

  4. Can't find each other

    Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network that keeps devices apart) and that Floor Plan is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on both. Then host on one and type its code on the other.

  5. The whole game

    The first three levels are free, with every way to play. "The whole game" is one purchase that opens levels 4 to 15, and every chapter added later, for good. There are no ads and no subscription. Family Sharing is on, so your household gets it too. The price is shown in the game.

  6. Your partner

    No. Whoever owns the whole game hosts, and the partner joins from the free download and plays any level the host picks. To host other people, they'd buy their own.

  7. Restore purchases

    Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. You can also tap any locked level on the City map and choose Restore purchases there.

  8. Progress

    Your stars and scores stay on this device. Deleting the app deletes them, and they don't move between devices. Updates keep them.

  9. Settings

    Settings has English and Spanish, left-handed touch controls (the stick on the right), vibration, goal hints, and a battery saver that runs lighter and at 30 frames a second.

  10. How scary

    Something stalks the house. It's tense and a little scary, with no gore.

  11. Something wrong

    Email mat.enrique.galicia@gmail.com with what happened (a screenshot helps), your device and iOS version, and the game's version (Settings › Credits).
