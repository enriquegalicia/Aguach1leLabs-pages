OverLanded — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

OverLanded is built local-first: your trip plans and journal entries are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Location

OverLanded requests location access while in use to narrate what's around you and help plan routes. Your location is used on your device and is not stored by Aguach1leLabs.

Camera

OverLanded requests camera access so you can identify plants and landmarks. Photos you scan for identification are sent directly from your device to your AI provider using an API key you provide and control yourself in Settings. Aguach1leLabs never sees, stores, or has access to these photos or your API key.

Your own AI key (BYOK)

OverLanded uses a "bring your own key" model for cloud AI features. Your API key is stored only in your device's Keychain, encrypted by iOS. It is never transmitted to Aguach1leLabs, logged, or included in any file we can access.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Third parties

OverLanded only communicates with your chosen AI provider, and only for the specific photo you choose to scan, using your own API key. That provider's handling of the request is governed by their own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

OverLanded is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
