OverLanded — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

OverLanded collects nothing. Your books, the copies of the photos you put in them and your answers are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login, so we never receive or store your data.

Photos

OverLanded asks for access to your photo library so it can read the date and location of the photos you choose and lay them out in a book. Your library is never changed; deleting a book doesn't touch your photos.

Google Maps Timeline files

If you import a Timeline export, the file is read on your iPhone to find the days you travelled and draw your route. It is not uploaded or shared.

Writing the story

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the story is written on the device. If you add your own OpenAI or Anthropic (Claude) API key in Settings, you can choose that writer instead: a short text summary of the day (the date, distance and number of stops, how many photos, and your answers) is sent from your device directly to that provider under your own account and their own privacy policy. Photos are never sent. Your key is stored only in your device's Keychain.

Purchases

"OverLanded Plus" is an optional one-time support purchase handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites

Children's privacy

OverLanded is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
