How do I make a book?

Tap Create and choose Pick photos, then select the photos from one day. OverLanded reads their dates, asks a few optional questions and drafts the book. Tap any page to change its layout, photos or words.

What is "From Google Timeline"?

If you keep location history in Google Maps, export it there (Settings › Location › Timeline › Export Timeline data) and import the file. OverLanded lists the days you visited more than one place and picks that day's photos for you. The file is read on your iPhone and not sent anywhere.

Do I need an API key?

No. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the story is written on the device; elsewhere it is built from your answers. Adding your own OpenAI or Claude key in Settings is optional and only offers another writer.

How do I print or share a book?

Open the book, tap Options › Export PDF, and send or print it from the share sheet.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

Your books live on your device. If you use iCloud backup for your phone, they're included like any other app's data. OverLanded itself has no cloud server.
