OverLanded

Your day, your route, a book

Coming soon

OverLanded turns one day out (a hike, a road trip, a day in a new city) into a photo book you can edit page by page and keep as a PDF.

What it does

  • Start from a day: pick the day's photos, or bring your Google Maps Timeline export and choose one of the days you really went somewhere
  • A first draft in seconds: the photos grouped into the moments of the day, a cover, pages laid out to fit them, and your route on a map page
  • The story, your way: answer a few short questions and a story page is written from them, on your iPhone
  • Edit every page: layouts, templates, themes, background colours, add, move or delete pages, undo anything
  • Keep it: export a landscape PDF ready to print or send

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your photos stay in your library.
