Sidequest

Somewhere new, right now

In development. Ask out loud for what you need (an errand, a recommendation, "somewhere new to go") and Sidequest finds it, routes you there and keeps you company on the way. No itinerary to plan first: the trip itself is the adventure.

What it will do

  • Voice requests, answered hands-free, on your phone's own mic and speaker or your car's audio
  • Routing handed off to Maps, with conversation along the way
  • CarPlay is an optional later surface, not a requirement
