MapWise

See a city's layers. Spot the deals.

Get it on the App Store · $1.99

Search any city, frame the neighbourhood you care about, and add the layers you need: schools, metro and bus lanes, government buildings, competitors, every registered business, and rental listings. MapWise shows where they meet and scores every spot and listing for what you're trying to do.

What it does

  • Start from a map and a search, and save any area of a city
  • Add layers only when you need them: Apple Maps places, OpenStreetMap government buildings and transit lines, Mexico's DENUE business directory, or your own files
  • Bring in rentals and sales by importing a spreadsheet, pasting a listing's link, or dropping them on the map
  • Ask proximity questions, like "listings within 400 m of a bus lane" or "cafés near a university"
  • Score spots and listings with a lens: open a business, buy to rent, or live here. Each score shows its reasons, and a heat map covers the whole area
  • Export everything as GeoJSON or CSV

No account. No tracking. Your maps stay on your phone.
