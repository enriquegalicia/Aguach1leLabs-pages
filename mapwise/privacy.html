MapWise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

MapWise is built local-first. Your areas, layers, pins, listings, and notes are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login. We never receive or store your data.

Your location

If you allow it, MapWise uses your approximate location (while the app is open) to center the map on where you are. Your location isn't stored in any history and isn't sent to Aguach1leLabs.

Data sources MapWise contacts, and what they receive

MapWise only contacts a service when you ask for a layer or search. Each request goes directly from your device to that provider. It never passes through us.

  • Apple Maps (search, places, address lookup): your search text and the area being searched. Covered by Apple's privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API (government buildings, transit lines, bus lanes, markets): the center and radius of the area. Operated by the OpenStreetMap community; see the OSMF privacy policy. If that server is busy, the same request goes to a public mirror of the same service run by VK (maps.mail.ru), under VK's own terms.
  • INEGI DENUE (Mexico's business directory; only if you add your own token): your search term, the area's center and radius, and your token. See INEGI's terms.
  • A listing page you paste: when you paste a link and tap "Read this listing", MapWise fetches that one page from that website to read its published price, size, and location, just like opening it in a browser.

MapWise does not crawl or bulk-download listing websites.

Your DENUE token

If you enter an INEGI DENUE token, it's stored in your device's Keychain and sent only to INEGI with your DENUE searches.

Files you import or export

CSV and GeoJSON files you import are read on your device. Exports are created on your device and go only where you choose to share them.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Children's privacy

MapWise is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
