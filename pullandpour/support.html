Questions from the counter

What is Pull & Pour?
A two-player co-op game. One barista pulls the shots and whisks the chocolate, the other steams the milk and pours the water, and every drink on the order needs both of you at the same moment.
Can I play alone?
Yes. On the City map, choose a bar and play as barista 1 or barista 2 with a bot barista who plays the other role.
How do we play together?
Each on your own iPhone or iPad, both on the same Wi-Fi. On the City map, choose a bar, then Play together: one phone each (on an iPad, Play online, on two devices). One of you taps Host a game and reads out the code; the other types it in and taps Join. On an iPad you can also share one screen: choose Play together on this iPad, half each.
We can't find each other's game
Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network that keeps devices apart) and that Pull & Pour is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on both. Then host on one and type its code on the other.
What does the purchase open?
The first three bars are free, with every way to play. "The Whole Game" is one purchase that opens bars 4 to 15, and every chapter added later, for good. There are no ads and no subscription. Family Sharing is on, so your household gets it too. The price is shown in the game.
Does my partner have to buy it too?
No. Whoever owns the whole game hosts, and the partner joins from the free download and plays any bar the host picks.
I bought it on another device, or reinstalled
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. You can also tap any locked bar on the City map and choose Restore purchases there.
Where is my progress?
Your stars and scores stay on this device. Deleting the app deletes them, and they don't move between devices. Updates keep them.
Language, left-handed controls, battery
Settings has English and Spanish, left-handed touch controls (the stick on the right), larger text, vibration, Helped mode, and a battery saver that runs lighter and at 30 frames a second.
Something's not working
Email mat.enrique.galicia@gmail.com with what happened (a screenshot helps), your device and iOS version, and the game's version (Settings › Credits).
