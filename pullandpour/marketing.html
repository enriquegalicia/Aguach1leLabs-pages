Behind the counter

One of you pulls the shots on a chrome lever machine and whisks the chocolate; the other steams the milk and pours the hot water. Every drink takes both of you at the same moment: a shot dies if it waits, the foam falls, and a half-made cappuccino goes cold on the counter.

The crew

  • Alone, with a bot barista who plays the other role.
  • Together on two iPhones or iPads on the same Wi-Fi: one hosts, the other types in a short code.
  • Or two of you on one iPad, half the screen each. Helped mode makes it gentle for a younger player with a grown-up.
  • Fifteen bars on the City map, from the espresso bar on the square to a dining car and the City's great coffee break.
