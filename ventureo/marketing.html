Ventureo

Remember everyone you meet at events, and actually follow up

Most of the people you meet at a conference never make it into any list, because typing a card in after a long day doesn't happen. Ventureo keeps a year of events and the people from each one in one place: photograph the card, score the lead, and get a follow-up draft you can send before you leave the venue.

Events, then people

  • Every event you're considering, applied to, confirmed for or attended, with its city, organizer and call for proposals
  • Each contact linked to the event where you met, so "who did I meet at that summit?" is one tap
  • A lead score and topics per contact, and a dashboard of hot leads and follow-ups due

A card in, a follow-up out

  • Photograph a business card and the fields fill themselves, read on your iPhone in English or Spanish
  • A follow-up draft from your notes and topics, written on the device; send it by email, Messages or any app
  • A reminder to follow up, so the draft doesn't sit there

Your data in your own spreadsheet

Optionally sync each workspace with a Google Sheet you own, through a small script you deploy in your own Google account. There is no Ventureo server in between.

No Ventureo account. No ads. No tracking. An OpenAI key is optional and only used if you allow it.
