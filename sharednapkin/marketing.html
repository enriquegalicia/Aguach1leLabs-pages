Sketch Together

Get it on the App Store · Free

Draw it, right now, with someone standing next to you — no Wi-Fi, no account, no server. Just two phones and a shared canvas.

What it does

  • Start a sketch and invite someone nearby — your strokes appear on their screen live, and theirs on yours
  • Full Apple Pencil support: pressure, tilt, undo, the system tool picker
  • On-device handwriting recognition turns a scribbled note into real text — nothing leaves your phone
  • Every sketch is saved so you can find it again
  • Export a sketch's image and recognized text anywhere

No ads. No tracking. No account required.
