PageWiser

Reading Companion

Point your camera at any page — a novel, a textbook, a menu in another language — and PageWiser helps you actually understand it, right there.

What it does

  • Scan any page and get guided reading help: simplified explanations, comprehension support, context for words or ideas that don't land
  • Read at your own pace with help that meets you where you are, not a generic summary
  • Built for real books and real pages, not just clean printed text

Runs on your own AI key (bring-your-own-key) — the page you scan goes straight from your phone to your own account, never through our servers, because we don't have any.
