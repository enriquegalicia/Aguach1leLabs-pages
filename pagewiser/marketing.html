PageWiser

Reading help for young readers

In the lab

PageWiser is being rebuilt to help kids read their own books: when a word gets in the way, tap it and keep reading.

What it will do

  • Scan a page of the book they're reading
  • Tap a word to hear it, see its syllables and get a meaning sized for kids
  • Keep the words they tapped in a Word Jar to practice later
  • Work in English and Spanish

The plan is for it to work on the device itself, for one price with no subscription.
