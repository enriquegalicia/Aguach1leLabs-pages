PageWiser — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

PageWiser is built local-first: your reading history and preferences are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Camera

PageWiser requests camera access so you can scan a page you're reading. The page image is sent directly from your device to OpenAI's API using an API key you provide and control yourself in Settings, for comprehension help and explanations. Aguach1leLabs never sees, stores, or has access to these photos or your API key.

Your own AI key (BYOK)

PageWiser uses a "bring your own key" model for cloud AI features. Your OpenAI API key is stored only in your device's Keychain, encrypted by iOS. It is never transmitted to Aguach1leLabs, logged, or included in any file we can access.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Third parties

The only third party PageWiser ever communicates with is OpenAI, and only for the specific page you choose to scan, using your own API key. OpenAI's handling of that request is governed by OpenAI's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

PageWiser is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
