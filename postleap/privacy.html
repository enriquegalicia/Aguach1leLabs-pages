PostLeap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server for PostLeap, there is no PostLeap account or login, and we never receive or store your data. What you create in PostLeap (accounts, posts, voice and style guidelines, and images) is stored on your device. It leaves your device only in the two ways below, both of which you set up yourself.

Your Google Sheet

If you connect an account to a Google Sheet, PostLeap sends that account's posts and guidelines to the Apps Script web app you deployed in your own Google account, and reads them back from it. That data is held by Google under your Google account and Google's terms. Aguach1leLabs has no access to your Sheet or your script. Images are not uploaded to the Sheet.

OpenAI, with your own key

AI drafting and image features use OpenAI's API with an API key you provide. When you use them, PostLeap sends OpenAI what the task needs: the post idea and the selected voice guideline to draft copy; the style prompt and post title or text to generate art; the reference photo you pick to describe its style. These requests go directly from your device to OpenAI under your own OpenAI account and OpenAI's terms. Aguach1leLabs never sees them.

Keys and tokens

Your OpenAI key and each account's sync token are stored in your device's Keychain. They are sent only to OpenAI and to your own script respectively.

Photos

PostLeap uses the system photo picker, so it sees only the photos you choose, never your library. A chosen reference image is saved inside the app.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email collection

Deleting your data

Deleting an account in PostLeap removes its posts and guidelines from your device and deletes its sync token. Rows already in your Google Sheet stay there until you delete them. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

Children's privacy

PostLeap does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
