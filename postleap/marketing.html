PostLeap

Draft, approve and schedule posts for every account, synced with a Google Sheet you own

In development

Your content calendar already lives in a spreadsheet: a teammate plans in it, and maybe a Make scenario publishes from it. PostLeap is the phone side of that sheet. Draft posts in each brand's voice, generate on-brand art, tick Ready and Approved, and everything syncs back to the same rows.

Your sheet stays the source of truth

Each account connects to its own Google Sheet through a small script you deploy in your own Google account, in about ten minutes. The app walks you through it and hands you the script. There's no PostLeap server in between, and only someone with your token can read or change the sheet.

What's inside

  • Several accounts or brands, each with its own calendar, voice and style
  • AI drafting from a one-line idea, in the voice you describe, with a sample post to match
  • On-brand post art from a style guideline, or from a reference photo PostLeap describes for you
  • Two-way sync that keeps the newer edit when you and a teammate change the same post
  • Edits made offline sync by themselves when you're back online
  • Works alongside Make: PostLeap never overwrites the columns your automations own

No PostLeap account. No ads. Bring your own OpenAI key for AI drafting and images. You pay OpenAI directly, and nothing is billed through the app.
