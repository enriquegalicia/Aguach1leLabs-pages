Common questions

Hometown and Blueprint: what's the difference?

Hometown is free: your real neighbourhood, read from the map, with the problems it probably has. Blueprint (one purchase) lets you draw a town of your own from open land.

I bought Blueprint on another device. How do I get it here?

Open the menu, choose Blueprint, and tap Restore purchases. Family Sharing works too.

Why does it say my street has these problems?

They're computed from the map: how paved and dense the street is, how far from parks, avenues and water. The “What we read” card says what was read and what was guessed.

My neighbourhood's map is missing buildings.

The map comes from OpenStreetMap, which volunteers keep up to date. If something is missing you can add it at openstreetmap.org.

Where is my town saved? Can I take it elsewhere?

On your device. Export the plan in the menu writes a GeoJSON file you can open in MapWise or Piazza, or share.

The text is too small.

Menu › Text: Standard, Large or Larger.
