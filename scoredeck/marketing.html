ScoreDeck

Live event judging and scoring, no internet needed

In development

ScoreDeck turns the iPhones and iPads at a judged competition into one scoring system: judges score on their own devices, a master console approves, and a big screen shows live standings to the crowd.

Built for judged events

  • BMX, skate, freestyle and dirt-jump contests, gymnastics, dance, or any format where judges score competitors across several categories
  • Judge: score each competitor on a simple keypad, one category at a time
  • Master: review and approve scores, manage the roster, advance the top riders to a final
  • Screen: an unattended, auto-rotating leaderboard for the crowd, overall and per category
  • Devices sync directly over the local network: no router, no internet, no account
  • A flag or photo for every competitor, and your event's logo and banner on the results screen
