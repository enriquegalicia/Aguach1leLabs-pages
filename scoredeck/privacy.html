ScoreDeck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

ScoreDeck collects nothing. Your events, competitors, judges, scores, photos and logos are stored only on your devices. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server and there is no account or login, so we never receive or store your data.

Syncing at the event

Devices at the same event exchange event data and scores directly with each other over the local network (Apple's peer-to-peer networking), only while ScoreDeck is open. Nothing passes through the internet or through us.

Optional AI images

If you add your own OpenAI API key in Settings, you can ask ScoreDeck to generate an event banner or rider avatar. When you do, a short text description of the picture is sent from your device directly to OpenAI under your own account and OpenAI's own policies; the finished image is saved on your device. Your key is stored in this device's Keychain. Without a key, nothing is ever sent to OpenAI.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites

Children's privacy

ScoreDeck is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
