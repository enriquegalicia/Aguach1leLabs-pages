Common questions

Do I need an OpenAI API key?

Only for scanning cookbook pages and receipts (photo → structured recipe/data). Go to Settings → AI and paste your own key — it's stored securely on your device and never leaves it except to talk directly to OpenAI. Get a key at platform.openai.com.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

Your recipes, shopping lists, and cooking history live on your device. If you use iCloud backup for your phone, they're included in that backup like any other app's data. GastroSnap itself has no cloud server.

Something's not working

Email a description of what happened (and a screenshot if you can) to mat.enrique.galicia@gmail.com — include your iOS version and the app version (Settings → About).
