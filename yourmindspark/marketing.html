Daily Puzzles

20 daily games, grouped so you can pick your mood: Logic & Numbers, Memory, Words, Speed & Reflex, and Strategy.

What it does

  • Sudoku Mini and Wordoku (same 6×6 grid, Wordoku spells a word when you solve it)
  • Memory Flash, Shape Recall, Card Match — pick your memory challenge
  • Word Scramble, Riddle Vault, Word Ladder, Hidden Word
  • Quick Math, Reflex Tap, Odd One Out, Color Rush, Quick Tap — beat the clock
  • Tic-Tac-Toe and Nim — go head-to-head against a real opponent, not a random-move bot

Every game adapts its difficulty to how you're actually doing — day one and day thirty feel different.
