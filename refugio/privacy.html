Refugio — Privacy Policy

Where to go when it hits.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Refugio helps you prepare for emergencies. It collects nothing: what you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The place you chose and its map, your backpack, your household's plan and your settings.

What the app never does

  • It has no account and asks for no name or email.
  • It collects no analytics, shows no ads and does not track you.
  • It sends nothing to Aguach1le Labs.

What goes out, and only when you ask

  • Search: Apple's own maps on iPhone and iPad; elsewhere OpenStreetMap's search (Nominatim) receives the text you type.
  • Your location: only if you tap “Use my location”, only while the app is open; it stays on your device.
  • The map: the chosen place's coordinates go to OpenStreetMap's map service, to read the streets and the places that may help.
  • Recent earthquakes: the app downloads the USGS's public list for the whole world; nothing about you or your place is sent.
  • Weather alerts (U.S. only): only your place's state (for example “CA”) is sent to the U.S. National Weather Service.

Those services see what any website sees (your device's internet address and the request), under their own policies.

Children

Refugio collects no personal data from anyone, children included.
