Questions about Refugio? The answers below cover most of them.

Common questions

Is Refugio an official alert service?

No. It helps you prepare and find places that may help. In an emergency, call your emergency number and follow your civil protection's instructions.

Why is a park first for an earthquake but missing for a flood?

Each kind of place suits each hazard differently: open ground is safest after an earthquake, and the worst place in a flood.

How are walking times worked out?

Along the streets on the map, at 4.5 km/h. A place beyond the streets read is marked “estimated”.

What does “room for about N people” mean?

An estimate from the place's outline on the map, after the Sphere humanitarian minimums. It helps you plan; it doesn't promise space.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes, once a place has been read. Your backpack and plan are always on the device.

Can I use it in Spanish?
