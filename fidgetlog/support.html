How do I play with my blob?

Tap to poke it, drag to stretch it, and hold still to squeeze it. Tilt your phone to slide it and shake it to make it wobble. Everything you do changes its Fidget DNA, which you can see in the strip under its name, in the Evolution tab and in the Jar.

How do I get Pop-Eggs?

Finish a whole sheet of bubble wrap (Feed → Bubble Wrap). Eggs can't be bought, and the odds for every rarity are listed in the Jar.

Where do my clips go?

When a clip finishes, Apple's own screen opens so you can trim, save or share it. It goes only where you send it; FidgetLog never uploads it or keeps a copy.

I bought Blob Lab on another device

Open Settings (the gear on the Blob tab) and tap Restore purchase.

I can't hear anything

Check that Pop sounds is on in Settings and your volume is up. Nap-time sounds (the Nap button on the Blob tab) keep playing with the silent switch on.

What is the Evolution tab?

FidgetLog keeps a count of how you touched your blob each day. The Evolution tab redraws it as it looked on each of those days, so you can see the day its spikes appeared or it turned stretchy. The count stays on your phone.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

Your blob and its history live on your device. If you use iCloud backup for your phone, they're included in that backup like any other app's data. FidgetLog itself has no cloud server.
