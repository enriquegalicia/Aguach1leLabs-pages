FidgetLog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

FidgetLog collects nothing. Your blob, its fidget history (including the per-day counts behind the Evolution tab) and the pets you hatch are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Clips and cards

FidgetLog never uses your camera or microphone. The Clip button uses Apple's screen recording (ReplayKit) to record only the app's own screen and sound, with the microphone off. When the clip ends, Apple's own screen lets you trim, save or share it. The clip goes only where you choose to send it; FidgetLog never uploads it or keeps a copy. The daily Blob Card is an image made on your phone and shared the same way.

Purchases

Blob Lab, the optional one-time upgrade, is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple ID.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No network calls of our own — your blob, its history and every sound stay on your device (only Apple's App Store is contacted, and only for Blob Lab)

Children's privacy

FidgetLog is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
