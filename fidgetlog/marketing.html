FidgetLog

Fidget & ASMR

A tension-release toy that's satisfying, not gross. An ASMR soundboard you can layer. And a place to record your own fidgeting, kept private on your phone.

What it does

  • Wrap — the real thing, digitally, pop by pop
  • Clear — a satisfying tension-release grid, kept tasteful and abstract on purpose
  • ASMR Soundboard — layer rain, tap, and hum sounds your way
  • Record — capture your own fidgeting or ASMR clips, saved privately on your device
  • Log — see your fidgeting history across every toy

Nothing you record ever leaves your phone. No feed, no sharing, no one watching — just something satisfying to do with your hands.
