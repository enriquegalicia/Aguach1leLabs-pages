Meet Gloop, a squishy pet whose body is a record of how you touch it. Tap it and it grows spikes, stretch it and it grows long, squeeze it and it goes round. No two players’ creatures end up alike.

What it does

  • A living blob: it dents, stretches, boings back, and follows your finger with its eyes
  • Fidget DNA: tappers grow spikes, stretchers grow long, squeezers go round, night fidgeters glow. A strip under its name shows the DNA moving while you play
  • Evolution: a page for every day you play, with your creature as it looked that evening and the moments that changed it
  • Food: bubble wrap (swipe to pop a whole row), soft clay bumps, and soap slicing
  • Pop-Eggs: finish a sheet to hatch one of 14 bizarre mini-blobs. Earned, never sold
  • Care and nap time: feed, clean, groom and polish it, then tuck it in to rain, soft taps or a low hum
  • Clips and daily cards to share your blob
