Nuvola — Support

Need help with Nuvola? Email Egalicia@avantleap.com and you'll hear back from the person who makes it.

How do I scan a room?

On an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro, tap Scan on the Scans screen and walk the room slowly, pointing at walls, floor and ceiling; the points appear over the camera. Rooms scan best one at a time, from 25 cm to 4.5 m away. Devices without LiDAR don't show Scan: use + > Open point file, or + > Sample scan.

I opened a point file and it looks wrong

Nuvola asks for the file's units when it opens one. If the room looks a thousand times too big or too small, open it again and pick another unit (metres, millimetres or feet).

Extraction takes a while

It runs on your device: about 15 seconds for the 161,000-point sample. A larger voxel under Rules is faster, at the cost of detail.

Where are my scans and packages?

In the Files app: On My iPhone (or iPad) > Nuvola > Scans for the scans, as PLY files, and > Packages for the Building Packages. Deleting the scans in Settings never deletes these files.

How do I open the model in Palazzo?

Approve the elements you want, then Open in Palazzo (or Share package, or Save to Files). Palazzo opens the package as a new building. The hand-off is part of Nuvola Pro.

My work after an update

Nuvola backs up your work before every update. Settings > Saved work lists the backups, restores one, and saves a copy to Files.

I bought Nuvola Pro on another device

Settings > Nuvola Pro > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I'd like a feature

Settings > Support Nuvola > Suggest a feature: tick the ideas you would use and add your own.

Something's not working

Email Egalicia@avantleap.com with what happened (a screenshot helps), your iOS version and Nuvola's version (Settings > About).
