Nuvola.

A scanned room, becoming a building model.

Scan a room with the LiDAR scanner of an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro, or open a point cloud from a survey scanner, and Nuvola finds the levels, walls, slabs and columns in the points. Then you decide what is right, approve the model, and hand it to Palazzo.

Scan

  • One point every 1.5 cm, measured by the LiDAR scanner, drawn over the camera as you walk.
  • Point files from survey scanners: E57, LAS, PLY, XYZ, PTS and CSV.
  • Everything runs on your device. No account, no upload.

Extract

  • Levels, walls with their measured thickness, slabs, sloped slabs and ramps, columns and curved walls.
  • A wall seen from one side only becomes a face: join it into a wall of the thickness you choose.
  • A report says what was measured and what a rule filled in. Nothing is guessed silently.

Approve

  • Check each element against the points and approve it as what it is: a slab can be a roof, a stair or a ramp.
  • Square, align, close corners and soften outlines within tolerances you set.
  • Make a wall, slab or column from the points you box.

Hand it on

  • The approved model as a Building Package, an open file that Palazzo opens as a building.
  • Share it, AirDrop it, or keep it in Files.

Free, and Pro once

Nuvola is free to scan, open files, extract and approve. Nuvola Pro is one in-app purchase, with no subscription: it adds the hand-off and unlimited scans (free: 3 kept at a time), on every device of your Apple Account. Tips, if you like Nuvola, unlock nothing and keep the work going.

What it needs

iOS 17.5 or later on iPhone or iPad. Scanning needs a LiDAR scanner: iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models, iPad Pro (2020 and later). Every device can open point files and the sample.

With Palazzo

Palazzo models the building in 3D, and opens what Nuvola hands it.
