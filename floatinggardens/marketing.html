Floating Gardens

Raise a garden on the lake that came back, weather the storm, sell what it grows.

Coming soon

Raise

Together you raise a garden bed on the lake, grow it and sell at the floating market. Each layer of the bed is mud you dredge together, plus reeds from the shore.

Weather

Willows on the north edge hold the bed when the storm comes.

Sell

Keep the water clean, harvest, row to market and fill the purse.

Ways to play

  • Alone, with a bot partner who plays the other role: friend 1 or friend 2.
  • Together on two iPhones or iPads on the same Wi-Fi: one hosts, the other types in a short code.
  • Or two of you on one iPad, half the screen each.
  • Fifteen places on the City map: four easy, four normal, four hard and three expert.

What it costs

  • The first three levels are free, with every way to play.
  • "The whole game" is one purchase that opens the other twelve, and every chapter added later.
  • Your partner plays free: they join your game from the free download.
  • No ads, no subscription, and no data collected. Family Sharing is on.

For phones and iPads

  • Touch controls built for thumbs, with a left-handed layout.
  • In English and Spanish.
  • Game Center leaderboards for every level and for the whole game.
  • A battery saver for long nights.
