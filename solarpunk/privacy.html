Solarpunk — Privacy Policy

Repair your street: shade, sun and water, together.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Solarpunk is a game about your own neighbourhood. This policy says what it does with your information. In short: it collects nothing, and your town stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The town you build, the place you chose, your settings and your notebook are saved in the app's own folder on your device. A plan you export is a file you send where you choose.

What the app never does

  • It has no account and asks for no name or email.
  • It collects no analytics, shows no ads and does not track you.
  • It sends nothing to Aguach1le Labs.

Reading a real place

  • Search: on iPhone and iPad the search uses Apple's own maps. Elsewhere, the text you type goes to OpenStreetMap's search (Nominatim).
  • Your location: only if you tap “Use my location”, only while the app is open, and it stays on your device.
  • The map: to read the streets and buildings, the chosen place's coordinates go to the map service the app uses (our copy of OpenStreetMap's map tiles, or OpenStreetMap's own servers). Nothing about you goes with them.

Those services see what any website sees (your device's internet address and the request), under their own policies. We keep no record of the places read.

Purchases

Blueprint is unlocked through Apple's App Store. We receive no payment details; Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

Solarpunk collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version comes with an update of the app, with its date above.
