RandoSquad

Fair Groups & Participation

Get it on the App Store · $1.99

RandoSquad takes the guesswork out of grouping students and calling on them fairly — add your roster once, and let it do the rest.

What it does

  • Random, balanced groups: generate 2 to 6 groups in one tap, weighted against who's already been grouped together and who's held which role
  • Fair participation, no favorites: pick a student to answer with one tap, weighted toward whoever's participated least, with a running count per student
  • Grade by group: save any group set as a named activity, grade each group, and see performance roll up into simple charts by activity or by student
  • Import or export your roster as a CSV — bulk-add students from a spreadsheet, or share a class list with another professor
  • Built for iPhone and iPad, laid out properly on whichever device you grab

Everything runs on-device — no account, no ads, no tracking, no internet connection required. Your roster is yours.
