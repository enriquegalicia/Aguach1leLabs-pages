Is my data backed up anywhere?

Your roster, groups, and grades live on your device. If you use iCloud backup for your phone, they're included in that backup like any other app's data. RandoSquad itself has no cloud server.

How do I bulk-add students or share a roster?

On the Roster screen, use the "Roster File" menu to import students from a CSV file, export your current roster as a CSV, or get a blank CSV template showing the expected columns (Student ID, First Name, Last Name, Email). This is handy for pulling a class list from a spreadsheet, or handing a roster to a co-teaching professor.

How do I share a group assignment?

On the Groups screen, the export button in the top-right shares the current group assignment as a CSV — which group each student is in, their role, and their ID.
