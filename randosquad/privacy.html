RandoSquad — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

RandoSquad is built local-first: your roster, groups, grades, and participation history are stored only on your device. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Roster CSV import/export

RandoSquad can import a roster from a CSV file, or export your roster or a group assignment as a CSV. These files are created and read directly on your device using the system's file picker and share sheet — nothing is uploaded to Aguach1leLabs or any third party. Where you send an exported file (email, another app, another professor) is entirely your choice.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Children's privacy

RandoSquad is a teacher-facing tool. Any student names or IDs you enter are entered by the teacher and stored only on the teacher's own device — RandoSquad does not knowingly collect information directly from children, and has no account system, ads, or network access through which it could.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
