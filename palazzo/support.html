Palazzo — Support

Need help with Palazzo? Email Egalicia@avantleap.com and you'll hear back from the person who makes it.

Where do I start?

Buildings > Start with the sample building opens a building to explore, or New building starts an empty one. Every tool is free on up to 2 buildings.

How do I draw a stair, a roof or a slab?

On the iPad the tools are in the toolbar; on the iPhone, under Create. Tap where it starts and where it goes; the prompt at the top says what the tool needs next. Slabs and roofs can be made from the walls round a room.

How do I open a scan from Nuvola?

In Nuvola, approve the model and tap Open in Palazzo (or share the package to Palazzo). Or in Palazzo: Buildings > + > From a Building Package…, and pick the file in Files.

Which extensions are free?

Kit, Digesto and Struttura. The others are marked Pro in Panels > Extensions.

My work after an update

Palazzo keeps your work as you go and backs it up before every update. Settings > Saved work lists the backups, restores one, and saves a copy to Files.

I bought Palazzo Pro on another device

Settings > Palazzo Pro > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I'd like a feature

Settings > Support Palazzo > Suggest a feature: tick the ideas you would use and add your own.

Something's not working

Email Egalicia@avantleap.com with what happened (a screenshot helps), your iOS version and Palazzo's version (Settings > About).
