Palazzo.

The building in 3D, in your hands.

Model a building on your iPad or iPhone: walls that join at their corners, slabs and roofs from the walls round a room, stairs and ramps, doors and windows, with typed lengths, snapping and angle steps, by finger or Apple Pencil.

Model

  • Walls that join, split, flip and offset; slabs, spaces and pitched roofs from the walls round a room.
  • Stairs and ramps from their foot the way they climb, railings by points, columns, beams, doors and windows.
  • Types with layers and materials, groups, assemblies and families; phases and design options.

Measure

  • Quantities by kind as you model: counts, areas and volumes.

Check and plan

  • Kit, Digesto (envelope and egress) and Struttura (frames and footings) are free.
  • Palazzo Pro adds clash checks, sequencing, the programme and its critical path, facade options with sun and energy, budgets, reinforcement intent, naming checks and more.

From scan and site

  • A Building Package from Nuvola (a room scanned with LiDAR) or from AvantLeap's Piazza (a site and its massing) opens as a new building.
  • Everything runs on your device. No account, no upload.

Free, and Pro once

Palazzo is free to model up to 2 buildings with every tool. Palazzo Pro is one in-app purchase, with no subscription: it adds unlimited buildings and every extension, on every device of your Apple Account. Tips, if you like Palazzo, unlock nothing and keep the work going.

What it needs

iOS 17.5 or later on iPhone or iPad. Apple Pencil works on the iPad, and isn't needed.

With Nuvola

Nuvola scans a room into a building model that Palazzo opens.
