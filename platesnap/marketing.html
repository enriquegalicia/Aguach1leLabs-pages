PlateSnap

AI Calorie Tracker

A photo of your plate, and PlateSnap tells you what's actually on it — ingredients, portions, macros, no logging by hand.

What it does

  • Snap or pick a photo of your plate — PlateSnap estimates ingredients, portions, and macros
  • Review and edit the estimate before it's saved — you're always in control of what gets logged
  • Every meal saved with its photo, building a real log over time

Runs on your own AI key (bring-your-own-key) — your plate photos go straight from your phone to your own account, never through our servers, because we don't have any.
