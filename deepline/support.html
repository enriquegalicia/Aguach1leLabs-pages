Down the line

The shift. A two-player co-op game. It's the night shift in the tunnels under the City. Bring the day's finds up in the lift: small ones take one pair of hands, the big one takes both of you. Something down here hunts by sound. Walk and it may hear you; stand still and it loses you. The water rises all night, so fill the quota and get both of you into the lift.

Sound. Play with headphones if you can: you'll hear where things are. Sound and music volumes are in Settings.

Fair warning. Deep Line is a horror game: dark tunnels and a creature that hunts you, with no gore. It's best with headphones.

Alone. Yes. On the City map, choose a level and play as digger 1 or digger 2 with a bot partner who plays the other role.

Two devices. Each on your own iPhone or iPad, both on the same Wi-Fi. On the City map, choose a level, then Play together: one phone each (on an iPad, Play online, on two devices). One of you taps Host a game and reads out the code; the other types it in and taps Join.

Lost each other? Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network that keeps devices apart) and that Deep Line is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on both. Then host on one and type its code on the other.

The purchase

The whole game. The first three levels are free, with every way to play. "The whole game" is one purchase that opens levels 4 to 15, and every chapter added later, for good. There are no ads and no subscription. Family Sharing is on, so your household gets it too. The price is shown in the game.

Your partner. No. Whoever owns the whole game hosts, and the partner joins from the free download and plays any level the host picks. To host other people, they'd buy their own.

Bought it before? Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. You can also tap any locked level on the City map and choose Restore purchases there.

Your saves, and trouble

Your progress. Your stars and scores stay on this device. Deleting the app deletes them, and they don't move between devices. Updates keep them.

Settings. Settings has seven languages, left-handed touch controls (the stick on the right), vibration, goal hints, and a battery saver that runs lighter and at 30 frames a second.
