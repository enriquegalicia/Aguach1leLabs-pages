The shift

It's the night shift in the tunnels under the City. Bring the day's finds up in the lift: small ones take one pair of hands, the big one takes both of you. Something down here hunts by sound. Walk and it may hear you; stand still and it loses you. The water rises all night, so fill the quota and get both of you into the lift.

Deep Line is a horror game: dark tunnels and a creature that hunts you, with no gore. It's best with headphones.

Who goes down

Alone, with a bot partner who plays the other role: digger 1 or digger 2.

Together on two iPhones or iPads on the same Wi-Fi: one hosts, the other types in a short code.

Fifteen places on the City map: four easy, four normal, four hard and three expert.

The price of the lift

"The whole game" is one purchase that opens the other twelve, and every chapter added later.

On your phone

Touch controls built for thumbs, with a left-handed layout. In English and Spanish. Game Center leaderboards for every level and for the whole game. A battery saver for long nights.
