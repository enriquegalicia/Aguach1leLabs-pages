Calco — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Calco is made by AvantLeap. This policy says what the app does with your information. In short: your captures and builds stay on your device unless you send them somewhere yourself.

What stays on your device

The captures you make with the camera and the LiDAR scanner, the mesh files you open, the builds made from them and the files they send out.

All of it is kept in the app's Documents folder on your device (shown in the Files app under On My iPhone or On My iPad > Calco > Builds). Your device's own backup (iCloud or a computer) may include it, under your Apple Account's settings.

Captures of people

A capture of a person is a 3D model of their body and the photos it was made from. The photos are used on your device to make the model and are deleted once the model is kept (if you leave before it is made, they wait on your device, out of Files, until you make the model or delete them); the model and its colours stay on your device. Capture a person only with their agreement. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it yourself.

What the app never does

  • It has no account, and asks for no name, email or other personal details.
  • It does not collect analytics, does not track you, and shows no advertising.
  • It sends nothing to AvantLeap.

The camera and the LiDAR scanner

Calco uses the camera and the LiDAR scanner only while you capture. A Detailed capture takes photos with depth to make the model on your device; a Quick capture keeps only the scanner's surface, without photos. Nothing is sent anywhere.

When you send something yourself

Files you share (through the share sheet, AirDrop or Files) go where you send them, under that app's or service's policy.

Suggestions

The ideas you tick under Suggest a feature are kept on your device. A suggestion is sent only when you send it yourself, from your own mail app or another app you choose, and holds only what you ticked or wrote and the app's version.

Purchases and tips

Calco Pro (one payment) and tips are bought through Apple's App Store. AvantLeap receives no payment details; Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase. The builds whose files went out free are counted on your device only, in its Keychain. The number of tips given is counted on your device only, to thank you.

Children

Calco is for everyone who builds. It collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version comes with an update of the app, with its date above.
