Calco.

Scan it. Build it again, to size.

Capture an object or a person with an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro, or open a 3D model, and Calco builds it again at the size you choose: in bricks, with steps and a parts list, or in cardboard, with cut files or templates to print.

Capture

  • An object or a person, with the camera and the LiDAR scanner: a guided walk-around makes a detailed model with its colours, on your device.
  • Quick capture: the scanner's surface in seconds, for big things.
  • Or open a 3D model from any app: PLY, OBJ, STL, GLB or USDZ.

Size it, to the millimetre

  • The model's real size, measured by the scanner, before anything is built.
  • Real size, a height, a scale such as 1:10, or as large as fits a box.
  • Tap two points you know the distance between, and everything rescales.
  • Every result says the size you asked for and the size it builds to.

In bricks

  • Standard bricks with 8 mm studs, large bricks at twice the size, or a system you measure yourself.
  • Only the types you have: start from basic, common or extended parts, and untick any type.
  • With extended parts, slopes smooth the steps and tiles the tops.
  • Hollow inside with pillars; the model's colours matched to a palette of 32 brick colours.
  • Every piece checked to rest on another, with supports where it would float.
  • Steps in 3D, layer by layer, with what to take from the box.

In cardboard

  • Stacked layers on dowels, each scored with where the next one sits, or slotted ribs that fit without glue.
  • Sized to the board you measured, laid out on the sheet you have.
  • Cut files for a knife cutter (SVG, DXF), or 1:1 templates to print, with a check square on every page.

Free, and Pro once

Calco is free to capture, size and build on screen, with the steps in 3D, and the files of your first 3 builds are free too. Calco Pro is one in-app purchase, with no subscription: the files of every build (the steps as PDF, the parts lists, the cut files and the templates), on every device of your Apple Account. Tips, if you like Calco, unlock nothing and keep the work going.

What it needs

iOS 17.5 or later on iPhone or iPad. Capturing needs the LiDAR scanner of an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro; any iPhone or iPad opens 3D models and builds them.
