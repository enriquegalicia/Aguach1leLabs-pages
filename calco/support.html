Calco — Support

Need help with Calco? Email Egalicia@avantleap.com and you'll hear back from the person who makes it.

Where do I start?

Builds > + offers Capture an object, Capture a person, Quick capture, Open a mesh file, and the sample figure, which shows the whole app without a scanner.

Why is Capture missing?

Capturing needs the LiDAR scanner of an iPhone Pro or iPad Pro. On any other iPhone or iPad, open a 3D model from Files or another app, or try the sample figure.

Capturing a person

Ask first. The person stands still for about 3 minutes while you walk slowly around them twice. The model stays on your device unless you share it.

How exact is the size?

The scanner measures the object; to check it, print the reference card (Settings), capture it beside the object and measure its 100 mm bar on the model. Every result shows the size asked and the size built, because bricks and board come in whole steps.

The printed templates are the wrong size

Print at 100%, not "fit to page", and measure the 100 mm check square on the page before cutting.

Which board thickness do I type?

Board sold as 3 mm varies. Stack ten sheets, measure the stack, divide by ten, and type that.

Where are my files?

In the Files app: On My iPhone (or iPad) > Calco > Builds, one folder per build with its model and files.

I bought Calco Pro on another device

Settings > Calco Pro > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I'd like a feature

Settings > Support Calco > Suggest a feature: tick the ideas you would use and add your own.

Something's not working

Email Egalicia@avantleap.com with what happened (a screenshot helps), your iOS version and Calco's version (Settings > About).
