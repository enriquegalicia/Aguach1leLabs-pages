Is my progress backed up anywhere?

Your runs and saved scores live on your device. If you use iCloud backup for your phone, they're included in that backup like any other app's data. Critical Path itself has no cloud server.

My leaderboard score isn't showing up

A few things explain this, all expected rather than broken:

  • Only a delivered run posts a score — an insolvent run has no profit worth ranking.
  • Game Center keeps your best score per board. A weaker run after a strong one won't replace it.
  • If you were offline or had just opened the app, the score is queued and sends the next time you bring Critical Path to the foreground with a connection.

You can check exactly what Game Center has and hasn't received from Settings → Diagnostics → Game Center.

Something's not working

From Settings → Diagnostics, tap Share to send a report — it includes what went wrong without sending anything else about you. Email it to mat.enrique.galicia@gmail.com along with a short description of what you were doing, your iOS version, and which scenario you were playing.
