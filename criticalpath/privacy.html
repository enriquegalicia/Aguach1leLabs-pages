Critical Path — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Critical Path collects nothing. Every run — your cash, your crew, your decisions, your saved scores — is stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login beyond the Game Center sign-in Apple itself provides — we never receive or store your data.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No network access beyond Apple's own Game Center, which you can decline and still play the full game

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center to post a leaderboard score, that sign-in and your alias are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. Critical Path only ever sees the score it submits and your public Game Center alias — nothing else about your Apple ID.

Crash and error reports

If the app crashes or hits an error, a report is written to your device so the problem can be found and fixed. It never leaves your device unless you choose to send it yourself, from Settings → Diagnostics → Share.

Children's privacy

Critical Path is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
