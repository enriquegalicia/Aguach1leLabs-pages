ViaticoSnap — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Every expense you log — project, event, concept, RFC, amounts — is stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData and standard iOS storage. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Scanning a receipt

ViaticoSnap can optionally use AI to read a receipt's fields for you. This uses your own OpenAI API key, entered by you and stored only in this device's Keychain — never sent to Aguach1leLabs. When you choose to scan a receipt, that receipt's image is sent directly from your device to OpenAI (standard HTTPS) to extract the fields, under your own key and subject to OpenAI's own privacy policy. This only happens when you explicitly tap to scan a receipt — manual expense entry never makes any network call at all.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No network access beyond the OpenAI receipt-scan request described above, and only when you choose to scan a receipt

Children's privacy

ViaticoSnap is a business-expense tool not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
