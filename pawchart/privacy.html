PawChart — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Everything you record in PawChart — your pets, their care plan and medications, every vital reading, checklist tick and note — is stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device storage. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, there is no account or login, and PawChart makes no network requests at all. We never receive or store your data.

Sharing with your vet

When you create a vet summary (PDF) or export your readings (CSV spreadsheet), the file is made on your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it — Mail, Messages, AirDrop, Files — under that app's own terms. PawChart never sends it anywhere by itself.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders for a checklist item, PawChart schedules local notifications on your device. They are not sent through any server.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email collection
  • No network access of any kind

Backups and deleting your data

If you use iCloud or computer backups for your phone, PawChart's data is included in them like any other app's. Deleting a pet in the app removes all of that pet's records from the device; deleting the app removes everything.

Children's privacy

PawChart does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
