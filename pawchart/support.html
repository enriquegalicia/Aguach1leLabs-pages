Where do I put the medication name and dose?

Go to the Care plan tab and tap the medication (or long-press it on the Today list and choose Edit name & dose). Enter the name, the amount per dose (2.5, or 1/2 for half a tablet), the unit, and how it's given. Copy it exactly from the prescription label — PawChart never suggests or calculates a dose. When you change a dose, the app remembers the date, and the vet summary shows it.

The cat refused her pill — what do I tick?

Long-press the item and choose Mark skipped / refused. Refusals are kept separately from doses given, and the vet summary counts them, because your vet will want to know.

Why is a reading flagged?

A warning triangle means the reading is outside the usual range for that species — or outside the target your vet gave you, if you entered one (Care plan → swipe a vital left → Target). It's a prompt to mention it to your vet, not a diagnosis. If your pet seems unwell, call your vet or an emergency clinic without waiting.

How do I take my pet's breathing rate or temperature?

Every vital has a short "how to measure" note under its entry box. For breathing rate: count breaths for 30 seconds while your pet sleeps, then multiply by two. For temperature, use a thermometer made for pets, and ask your vet to show you how.

Celsius or Fahrenheit, kilograms or pounds?

Care plan → Units. Your history is converted on screen; nothing you've recorded changes.

Can two people use it for the same pet?

Not yet — everything lives on one device. Shared care between family members is planned.

Is my data backed up?

PawChart has no cloud server. Your data is included in your phone's normal iCloud or computer backup. You can also export every reading as a spreadsheet at any time (Care plan → Share with your vet).
