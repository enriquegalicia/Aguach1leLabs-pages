PawChart

A nursing chart for the pet you're caring for

When a pet is ill, every day becomes a list: pills at eight, fluids at seven, warm the bed, weigh her on Sunday, count the litter clumps. And every vet visit starts with the same question — "how has she been?" PawChart keeps the list and the answer in one place.

A care plan, not just a diary

Tell PawChart what your vet is treating — hyperthyroidism, kidney disease, low body temperature, diabetes, heart disease, recovery from surgery, or senior comfort care — and it suggests the daily checklist a vet would write. Add each medication's name, dose and how it's given, set the times, and tick each one off as done or refused.

What's inside

  • A daily care checklist grouped by morning, afternoon, evening and night, with reminders
  • Medication name, dose, unit and route — with the date the dose last changed
  • Thirteen vitals: weight, temperature, breathing rate, heart rate, blood pressure, water, food, urination, vomiting, energy, stool, glucose and quality of life
  • Charts with the usual range shaded in — or your vet's own target for your pet
  • A heads-up when a reading is out of range or weight drops more than 5%
  • A one-tap PDF summary for your vet, plus every reading as a spreadsheet
  • Cats, dogs, and other pets — as many as you're caring for

In English and Spanish.

No account. No ads. Nothing leaves your phone unless you send it. PawChart is a care diary, not a veterinarian — always follow your vet's advice.
