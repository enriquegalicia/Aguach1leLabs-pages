Piazza — Support

Need help with Piazza? Email Egalicia@avantleap.com and you'll hear back from the person who makes it.

Where do I start?

Projects opens on the sample data-centre campus: open its proposal to see the site and its buildings in 3D. New project starts an empty one, and New project from an address starts one on a place you find on the map.

How do I use a site boundary I already have?

Share a GeoJSON or KML file to Piazza from another app, or open a project and tap Import site. Piazza asks for the setback, then draws the site.

How do I hand a proposal to Palazzo?

Export > Building Package, then share it to Palazzo, which opens it as a new building. The exports are part of Piazza Pro.

How many projects are free?

Three, the sample included. Piazza Pro keeps as many as you make. Settings > Reset to the sample starts again from the sample.

Where are my exports?

In the Files app: On My iPhone (or iPad) > Piazza holds the Building Packages, GeoJSON, CSV and pictures.

My work after an update

Piazza backs up your work before every update. Settings > Saved work lists the backups, restores one, and saves a copy to Files.

I bought Piazza Pro on another device

Settings > Piazza Pro > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I'd like a feature

Settings > Support Piazza > Suggest a feature: tick the ideas you would use and add your own.

Something's not working

Email Egalicia@avantleap.com with what happened (a screenshot helps), your iOS version and Piazza's version (Settings > About).
