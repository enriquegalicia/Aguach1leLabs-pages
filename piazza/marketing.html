Piazza.

Where a building starts: the site, the massing and the numbers.

Start a project from an address or a site file, draw buildings, zones and roads on it in 3D or in plan, compare proposals by their numbers, and hand the building to Palazzo.

Site

  • Start a project from an address: find it on the map, set the site's size, and reshape it later.
  • Or open a site boundary from another app, GeoJSON or KML, with its setback.

Proposals

  • Draw buildings, zones, roads, rails, trees, lines, surfaces and volumes in 3D or in plan, with exact dimensions.
  • Move, turn, copy, reshape corners, snap to edges, undo and redo.
  • Several proposals per project; every save is a version you can open, compare and restore.

Metrics

  • Areas, floor areas, heights and counts for each proposal, side by side.
  • Colour elements by any property; sections, saved views and a picture of the view.

Hand it on

  • The proposal as a Building Package that Palazzo opens as a building.
  • GeoJSON and the metrics as CSV, kept in Files and shared.
  • Everything runs on your device. No account, no upload.

Free, and Pro once

Piazza is free to start projects, draw proposals and compare their metrics, with up to 3 projects, the sample included. Piazza Pro is one in-app purchase, with no subscription: it adds the exports (the Building Package for Palazzo, GeoJSON and the metrics CSV) and unlimited projects, on every device of your Apple Account. Tips, if you like Piazza, unlock nothing and keep the work going.

What it needs

iOS 17.5 or later on iPhone or iPad. New project from an address needs a connection, to search Apple Maps.

With Palazzo

Palazzo opens Piazza's Building Package as a new building, to model it in 3D.
