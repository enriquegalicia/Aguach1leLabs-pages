Piazza — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Piazza is made by AvantLeap. This policy says what the app does with your information. In short: your work stays on your device unless you send it somewhere yourself.

What stays on your device

The projects you make, their sites, the proposals drawn on them and every saved version.

All of it is kept in the app's own folders on your device: its working data in Application Support, and the files it makes for you in its Documents folder (shown in the Files app under On My iPhone or On My iPad > Piazza): Building Packages, GeoJSON, the metrics as CSV and pictures of the view. Backups of your work, made before an update of the app, are kept there too. Your device's own backup (iCloud or a computer) may include them, under your Apple Account's settings.

What the app never does

  • It has no account, and asks for no name, email or other personal details.
  • It does not collect analytics, does not track you, and shows no advertising.
  • It sends nothing to AvantLeap.

When the app reaches something on your behalf

  • New project from an address sends the words you type to Apple Maps to find the place, under Apple's privacy policy. Piazza keeps only the site made from it.
  • The base layer's map tiles come from the address you set in Settings. Piazza has no tile provider of its own: the provider you choose receives the requests for the tiles it shows.
  • A Piazza server, or a host for extension panels, is reached only when you set one in Settings. Your work is then sent to the server you entered, which you or your organisation runs. Any access key you enter is kept in the device's Keychain.

When you send something yourself

  • Files you share (through the share sheet, AirDrop or Files) go where you send them, under that app's or service's policy.
  • Save Image adds a picture of your proposal to your photo library, after asking your permission once.

Suggestions

The ideas you tick under Suggest a feature are kept on your device. A suggestion is sent only when you send it yourself, from your own mail app or another app you choose, and holds only what you ticked or wrote and the app's version.

Purchases and tips

Piazza Pro (one payment) and tips are bought through Apple's App Store. AvantLeap receives no payment details; Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase. The number of tips given is counted on your device only, to thank you.

Children

Piazza is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version comes with an update of the app, with its date above.
