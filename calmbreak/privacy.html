CalmBreak — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

CalmBreak is built local-first: your resist logs, minutes saved, and Screen Time app selections are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData and Screen Time frameworks. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Screen Time / Family Controls

If you grant Screen Time access, CalmBreak uses Apple's own FamilyControls and ManagedSettings frameworks to let you choose apps to limit and shield them. This information is managed entirely by Apple's on-device Screen Time system — Aguach1leLabs never sees which apps you've selected or how you use them.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Children's privacy

CalmBreak is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
