You build the tower you're standing on, one step at a time. One of you holds each beam while the other bolts it, fast: a late bolt is loose. When a tremor comes, brace. If one of you falls, the rope between you catches them; hold Jump to climb back. Top out before the big quake.

On the job

Alone, with a bot partner who plays the other role: ironworker 1 or ironworker 2.
Together on two iPhones or iPads on the same Wi-Fi: one hosts, the other types in a short code.
Fifteen places on the City map: four easy, four normal, four hard and three expert.
