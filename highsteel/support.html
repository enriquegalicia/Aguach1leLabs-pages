On the column

What is High Steel?
A two-player co-op game. You build the tower you're standing on, one step at a time. One of you holds each beam while the other bolts it, fast: a late bolt is loose. When a tremor comes, brace. If one of you falls, the rope between you catches them; hold Jump to climb back. Top out before the big quake.
Can I play alone?
Yes. On the City map, choose a level and play as ironworker 1 or ironworker 2 with a bot partner who plays the other role.
How do two of us play?
Each on your own iPhone or iPad, both on the same Wi-Fi. On the City map, choose a level, then Play together: one phone each (on an iPad, Play online, on two devices). One of you taps Host a game and reads out the code; the other types it in and taps Join.
We can't find each other's game
Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network that keeps devices apart) and that High Steel is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on both. Then host on one and type its code on the other.

The whole game

What does the purchase open?
The first three levels are free, with every way to play. "The whole game" is one purchase that opens levels 4 to 15, and every chapter added later, for good. There are no ads and no subscription. Family Sharing is on, so your household gets it too. The price is shown in the game.
Does my partner pay too?
No. Whoever owns the whole game hosts, and the partner joins from the free download and plays any level the host picks. To host other people, they'd buy their own.
I bought it before
Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. You can also tap any locked level on the City map and choose Restore purchases there.

Your settings and saves

Where are my stars?
Your stars and scores stay on this device. Deleting the app deletes them, and they don't move between devices. Updates keep them.
Language, controls, battery
Settings has seven languages, left-handed touch controls (the stick on the right), vibration, goal hints, and a battery saver that runs lighter and at 30 frames a second.
