High Steel — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

High Steel doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. Aguach1leLabs runs no server for it and never receives anything about you or how you play.

Never on this site

  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email collection
  • No camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos
  • No internet connection of our own: the only online calls are Apple's, the App Store for the purchase and Game Center if you're signed in

What it keeps, and what travels

On your device
Your stars, best scores, settings, and whether you own the whole game are saved only on this device, in the app's own storage. Deleting the app deletes them. They aren't synced to iCloud or anywhere else.
Playing together on the same Wi-Fi
To play with a partner on another iPhone or iPad, High Steel asks for Local Network access. One device hosts and shows a short code; the other joins with it. The two devices then send each other only what the game needs (where the characters are, the buttons pressed) directly over your Wi-Fi. Nothing goes over the internet and nothing reaches us. If you decline, you can still play alone with a bot partner.
Game Center
If you're signed in to Game Center, High Steel posts your best score on each level, and your total, to Apple's leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive nothing from it. The game works the same if you're not signed in.
The purchase
"The whole game" is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; the game is only told whether you own it, so it can open the levels. We never see your payment details or your Apple Account.

The rest

Children's privacy
High Steel doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
