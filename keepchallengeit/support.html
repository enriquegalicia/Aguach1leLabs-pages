Is my streak backed up anywhere?

Your challenges, streak, and history live only on your device. If you use iCloud backup for your phone, they're included in that backup like any other app's data. KeepChallengeIt itself has no cloud server.

I missed a day — is my streak gone?

Yes. For now a streak counts strictly. A rest-day option that protects your streak is planned but not built yet.

Something's not working

Email a description of what happened (and a screenshot if you can) to mat.enrique.galicia@gmail.com — include your iOS version and the app version (Settings → About).
