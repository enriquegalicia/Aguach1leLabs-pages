Tavola — Support

Need help with Tavola? Email Egalicia@avantleap.com and you'll hear back from the person who makes it.

Where do I start?

Drawings opens with the sample set: open "site" to see a drawing on its sheet. Drawings > + > New drawing starts an empty one in the units you choose.

How do I open a DXF?

Share it to Tavola from another app, or tap Drawings > + > Open a drawing or a DXF and pick it in Files. Tavola opens it beside any drawing of the same name, never over it.

Does Tavola open DWG?

Not yet. Save the drawing as DXF in the program that made it, then open the DXF in Tavola.

How do I print a sheet?

Panels > Layouts shows the drawing's sheets as paper, with the title block. PDF of one sheet or of the whole set is part of Tavola Pro.

Where are my files?

In the Files app: On My iPhone (or iPad) > Tavola holds the drawings, DXF, PDF and CSV.

My work after an update

Tavola backs up your work before every update. Settings > Saved work lists the backups, restores one, and saves a copy to Files.

I bought Tavola Pro on another device

Settings > Tavola Pro > Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I'd like a feature

Settings > Support Tavola > Suggest a feature: tick the ideas you would use and add your own.

Something's not working

Email Egalicia@avantleap.com with what happened (a screenshot helps), your iOS version and Tavola's version (Settings > About).
