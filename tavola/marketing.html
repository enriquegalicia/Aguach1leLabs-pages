Tavola.

The drawing board, in your hands.

Draw any technical drawing on your iPad or iPhone: 25 drafting tools, layers, sheets with your office's title block, and DXF in and out.

Draft

  • Line, polyline, rectangle, circle, text and tags; move, copy, rotate, scale, mirror, array and offset.
  • Trim, extend, fillet, chamfer, break, join and explode; distance and area as you go.
  • Typed lengths on the command line, snapping to end points, window and crossing selection.
  • Layers on and off, the layer to draw on, colours and linetypes as the drawing has them.

Sheets

  • Sheets as paper: viewports at a scale, schedules, revisions.
  • Your office's own title block, filled from each sheet's number, name, scale and date.
  • PDF of one sheet or the whole set, in colour or not (Tavola Pro).

Open and share

  • Open DXF drawings from any CAD program, and share yours as DXF or as a Tavola drawing.
  • Take a drawing off by category and type, and share the quantities as CSV (Tavola Pro).
  • Everything runs on your device. No account, no upload.

Review a set

  • Go through a whole set page by page: notes, sign-offs, what changed since the last review (Tavola Pro).

Free, and Pro once

Tavola is free to draft, open and share drawings and DXF, as many as your device holds. Tavola Pro is one in-app purchase, with no subscription: it adds sheets as PDF with your office's title block, the takeoff as CSV and the review of a whole set, on every device of your Apple Account. Tips, if you like Tavola, unlock nothing and keep the work going.

What it needs

iOS 17.5 or later on iPhone or iPad.
