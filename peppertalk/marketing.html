Assertiveness training you do out loud

Say what you mean — kindly and clearly. Learn a named technique, then practice it in a real conversation with a character who pushes back: saying no, asking for a raise, setting a boundary, taking criticism without folding or snapping.

What it does

  • 28 days, about 10 minutes each: one technique, one drill, one roleplay, one real-life mission
  • 17 techniques from assertiveness training: I-statements, the DESC script, broken record, fogging, the kind firm no, empathic assertion and more
  • Roleplays that react: hesitate and the other person pushes harder; snap and they get defensive; be clear and kind and they come around
  • Phrase Coach: catches “does that make sense?”, “sorry to bother you”, “I just think…” and harsh words like “you always”, then suggests a warm, clear version
  • Progress you can feel: streaks, scores, and the habits that are fading
  • English and Spanish, end to end — lessons, voices, speech recognition and feedback
  • Private: no account, no ads, no tracking; your practice stays on your iPhone

Most apps give you tips. PepperTalk gives you reps.
