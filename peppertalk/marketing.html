PepperTalk

Speaking Coach

A real conversation, not a solo drill. PepperTalk puts you across from an AI character who reacts — interview rehearsal, tough conversations, thinking out loud — and helps you get sharper at saying what you mean, out loud, in the moment.

What it does

  • 28 days of structured practice, ~9–12 minutes a day, building from foundations to real pressure scenarios
  • A live back-and-forth with a reactive AI persona — not a script you read at
  • Works hands-free on your phone's own mic/speaker, any headphones, or the car

Most speaking apps score a solo recording. PepperTalk actually talks back.
