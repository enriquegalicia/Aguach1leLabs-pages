PepperTalk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PepperTalk is built local-first: your practice history, scores and progress through the 28-day program are stored only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Microphone and speech recognition

PepperTalk asks for microphone access only so it can hear your answers while you practice out loud. What you say is turned into text with Apple's Speech framework. On iPhones and languages that support it, this happens entirely on your device. If your iPhone doesn't support on-device recognition for your language, Apple's speech recognition service processes the audio under Apple's privacy policy. PepperTalk never records or saves audio, and the text of your answers stays on your device. You can always type your answers instead.

Conversation partner and coach's notes

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later), the character you practice with and the written coach's note use Apple's on-device language model. It runs on your iPhone; your conversation is not sent to Aguach1leLabs or any other company. On other iPhones, the partner follows built-in scripts that run on the device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, PepperTalk schedules a local notification on your iPhone. No notification service or server is involved.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Deleting your data

Settings › Reset all progress deletes every practice session. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children's privacy

PepperTalk is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
