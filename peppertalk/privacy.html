PepperTalk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

PepperTalk is built local-first: your practice history and progress through the program are stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework. Aguach1leLabs does not operate a server, and there is no account or login — we never receive or store your data.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

PepperTalk requests microphone access to hear what you say during a practice session, and uses Apple's on-device Speech framework to transcribe it. By default, the AI character you practice with runs entirely on your device (local AI). If you choose to enable higher-quality cloud responses in Settings, your spoken text is sent directly from your device to your own AI provider using an API key you provide and control yourself — Aguach1leLabs never sees, stores, or has access to that audio, its transcript, or your API key.

Your own AI key (BYOK, optional)

Cloud AI in PepperTalk is opt-in and uses a "bring your own key" model. Your API key is stored only in your device's Keychain, encrypted by iOS. It is never transmitted to Aguach1leLabs, logged, or included in any file we can access.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No sale of your data — we don't have a way to collect it in the first place

Third parties

PepperTalk only communicates with a third party if you choose to enable cloud AI, and only for that session, using your own API key. That provider's handling of the request is governed by their own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

PepperTalk is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
