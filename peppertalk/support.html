PepperTalk — Support

Need help with PepperTalk? Email mat.enrique.galicia@gmail.com and I'll get back to you.

Do I need an API key?

No — PepperTalk works out of the box with on-device AI. If you want higher-quality cloud responses, go to Settings → AI and paste your own key; it's stored securely on your device and never leaves it except to talk directly to your chosen provider.

Is my data backed up anywhere?

Your practice history lives on your device. If you use iCloud backup for your phone, it's included in that backup like any other app's data. PepperTalk itself has no cloud server.

Something's not working

Email a description of what happened (and a screenshot if you can) to mat.enrique.galicia@gmail.com — include your iOS version and the app version (Settings → About).
